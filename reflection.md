Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

Throughout this mission, Amazon Web Services (AWS) was the cloud platform that stood out to me the most. I was impressed by the wide variety of services it offers for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and other cloud requirements. Services such as Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, and Amazon RDS can support a wide range of applications and business needs.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) share many similar features. All three platforms provide virtual machines, object storage, databases, identity and access management, and Kubernetes services. They also allow organizations to scale their resources according to their needs without having to purchase and maintain large amounts of physical hardware. While the services may have different names and specific features, they address many of the same common cloud computing requirements.

Business requirements play an important role when choosing a cloud provider because each organization has unique goals and needs. For instance, a business that heavily relies on Microsoft products may find Azure more suitable, while an organization focused on artificial intelligence and machine learning may prefer GCP. Startups may place greater importance on affordability and scalability, whereas larger companies may prioritize security, reliability, performance, and worldwide availability.

Working as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that selecting a cloud provider involves more than simply choosing the most well-known platform. I learned how to evaluate an organization's requirements and identify the cloud services that best match those needs. I also developed a better understanding of how AWS, Azure, and GCP compare in terms of their features and capabilities.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has also improved as a result of this mission. I now have well-organized documentation containing my activities, cloud service comparisons, client recommendations, decision matrices, and Linux investigation. I also gained experience using GitHub to organize and present my work in a professional manner.

Overall, this mission helped me understand how cloud computing concepts can be applied to real-world business situations and strengthened my ability to evaluate cloud platforms based on specific organizational requirements.
